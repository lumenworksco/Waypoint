Privacy Policy for WayPoint

Last updated: April 2026

WayPoint is an Android app developed by Florian Braun (Lumen Studios). This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data We Collect

WayPoint accesses your device's location (GPS) to display your position on the map and to calculate distances to your saved waypoints. Location data is used only within the app and is never transmitted to any server or third party by WayPoint itself.

Data Storage

Waypoints you save are stored locally on your device only. We do not use any cloud storage, databases, or external servers. Your data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

WayPoint uses the following third-party services:

  • OpenStreetMap – map tile images are loaded from OpenStreetMap tile servers. These requests may include your IP address as part of standard internet communication. See the OpenStreetMap Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services Location – used to determine your device's position. Location processing is handled on-device by Google Play Services. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

WayPoint requests the following Android permissions:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION – to show your precise position on the map
  • ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION – as a fallback for approximate location

You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings. Without location permission, the app's core functionality will be limited.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit any personal data to third parties. No analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used in this app.

Children's Privacy

WayPoint is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
